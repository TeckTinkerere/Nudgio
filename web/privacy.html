Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

The Nudgio app

Nudgio does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send anything off your device.

What is stored on your device

Your reminders, imported media, thumbnails and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device only. No other app can read them, and uninstalling Nudgio deletes them.

One exception, and only when you ask for it: the Library's Save a copy to gallery action writes a copy of that one item into your device's own Pictures, Movies or Music folder, under Nudgio. Copies saved that way behave like any other photo or video on your phone — other apps with media access can see them, and they stay on the device after Nudgio is uninstalled. Nudgio never does this on its own.

Backups

If you export a backup, Nudgio creates a ZIP file that you choose how to save or share. It contains your reminders and settings and the photos, videos and audio they play, so it can be large and it is as private as the media inside it. Nudgio never uploads it anywhere, but anyone who receives that file can open and view everything in it — share it with care. Restoring a backup on another device brings the media back with the reminders.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications — to show a reminder when it is due.
  • Exact alarms and full-screen alerts — to fire on time and show the alarm over the lock screen.
  • Run at boot — to re-register your reminders after a restart.
  • Vibrate, wake lock, foreground service (media playback) — to ring and play your media while an alarm is active.

Nudgio does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or your whole photo library. Media is only read from files you pick yourself.

This website

The download page checks GitHub for the latest release, which means GitHub sees your IP address as it would for any visit to github.com. If you join the optional waitlist, the email address you enter is stored on a database hosted by Upstash and is used only to tell you about Nudgio releases. Your IP address is used briefly to rate-limit sign-ups. To have your address removed, contact us at the address below.

If you pick a light or dark theme, that choice is saved in your browser's own storage under nudgio-theme and never leaves your device. Leaving the theme on system saves nothing. There are no cookies and no analytics. The download page loads one web font from Google Fonts; that and the release check above are the only requests either page makes off this site.

Children

Nudgio does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
