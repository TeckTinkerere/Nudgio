About test alarms
After enabling alarm access, you can try a test alarm in 15 seconds.

No false “Ready” state. The screen explains the consequence and provides one repair action. Test alarm becomes available after permission is granted.

Platform-native where it matters

System typography, native date/time controls, standard navigation and OS-owned alarm presentation. App content uses calm, readable grouped surfaces.

Accessible by construction

44 pt minimum hit areas, 56 pt primary playback actions, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels, strong text contrast and no gesture-only essential action. The plan requires real accessibility tests.

Truthful at every step

Saved, scheduled and heard are different facts. Missing media, denied permission and failed edits have specific recovery states. No sync, accounts, payments or background media playback in this release.
