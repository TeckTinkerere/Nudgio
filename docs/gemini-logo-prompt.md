# Gemini prompt — Nudgio logo

Create an original app-logo symbol for **Nudgio**, a calm personal reminder app that helps people return to meaningful actions and saved media.

**Concept:** A gentle nudge becoming forward movement. Build a compact abstract lowercase **n** from one smooth, bent, rounded ribbon. Add one short, clean curved line near its upper-right opening and one small apricot sphere at the end of that line. The sphere should feel like a subtle prompt setting something in motion. Integrate these into one recognisable silhouette, not three unrelated decorations.

**Geometry:** Simple shapes, generous negative space, consistent rounded edges and a strong silhouette. Use only a few broad forms. Keep the ribbon readable at app-icon size; the curved line must remain thick enough to survive reduction. Let the negative space do some of the work.

**Colour palette — use these exact base colours:**
- Main ribbon: ink blue **#2D4DB5**.
- Small sphere/accent: apricot **#E9B58E**.
- Background: warm ivory **#F7F4EE**.
- Optional highlight: paper **#FFFEFA**.
- Optional subtle shadow: deep ink **#202638**, low opacity.

**3D treatment:** Restrained, shallow 3D extrusion with soft bevels and a matte ceramic finish. Mostly front-facing, with only enough depth to give the shapes a tactile quality. Soft studio lighting from the upper left; a small, soft contact shadow. Maintain clean, nearly vector-like edges. The symbol must also work as a flat, single-colour silhouette.

**Composition:** One centred logo symbol on a plain warm-ivory square canvas, 1024×1024 or higher. Keep the complete mark within the central 60% of the canvas so it can later be adapted to Android and iOS icon masks. Strong visual balance and enough breathing room. Do not bake in a rounded-square app-icon border or a phone mockup.

**Avoid:** Text or lettering outside the abstract symbol, wordmarks, slogans, literal bells or alarm clocks, tiny clock ticks, generic play-button triangles, checkmark logos, multiple orbit rings, extra spheres, complex gradients, teal/green, neon, chrome, glass, dramatic perspective, heavy shadows, background patterns or decorative scenes. Do not imitate an existing app logo.

**Output:** One finished symbol, not a presentation sheet or a collage of variations. Aim for a distinctive, warm and dependable identity that remains recognisable at 24–48 pixels.

After selecting a design, request a flat vector-style version and a transparent-background symbol as separate follow-ups. A generated image is a concept; manually verify colour values, small-size readability and adaptive-icon exports before shipping it.
